# Corner Bakery: where does our revenue come from?
*5-slide deck · data: orders.csv, 1 Aug – 31 Oct 2026 (simulated, anonymised)*

## Slide 1 — The question
A neighbourhood bakery sells through three channels: walk-in, delivery app, phone.
Question: **which days, products and channels earn the most, and what are we losing?**

## Slide 2 — The data and the method
- 1,220 orders over 92 days; customers appear only as codes (C001–C140).
- Revenue counts **completed orders only**: 1,110 orders, **$8,590.81**.
- Cancelled (84) and refunded (26) orders are excluded and shown separately.

## Slide 3 — What earns the money
- **Cakes: $3,612 (42.0% of revenue)**, the largest category by far.
- **Saturday: $2,187 (25.5%)**; Fri–Sun together bring 61.7%.
- Wednesday is the weakest day: $614 (7.1%).
- Walk-in 52.4%, delivery app 38.4%, phone 9.3%.

## Slide 4 — What we lose
- 110 of 1,220 orders (9.0%) were cancelled or refunded.
- That is **$618.23** of order value that did not become revenue.
- The data does not say *why* orders were cancelled.

## Slide 5 — Next steps (hypotheses to test, not findings)
1. Test a Wednesday offer (e.g. a pastry + drink set). *Hypothesis: weekday demand can be lifted.*
2. Plan cake production around Fri–Sun peaks. *Hypothesis: fewer sold-out cakes.*
3. Add a "cancel reason" field to the order system, so slide 4 can be explained.
